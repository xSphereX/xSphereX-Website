Three Lines
About Focus.

You can’t hold every star at once, so pick one and move toward it. Three lines, written late at night, about exactly that.

try it the whole sky, at once

flip the switch to see what it’s really about

by sphere Melody
Sometimes one may feel the urge to reach for all the stars. But to draw closer, one must learn to focus on a single one. That realization may come from something as simple as a melody.
